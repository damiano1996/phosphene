# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) (© the Contributor Covenant authors, CC BY 4.0),
in short form.

**Our pledge.** We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**What we expect.** Be kind and patient. Disagree with the idea, not the person. Give and accept feedback with grace. Take responsibility for mistakes. Focus on what is best for the project and
the people who use it.

**What is not acceptable.** Sexualised language or attention; trolling, insults, personal or political attacks; harassment, public or private; publishing someone's private information without
permission; anything else that would reasonably be thought inappropriate in a professional setting.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for
behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful. This applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.

**Reporting.** Report abuse privately to the maintainers through GitHub: open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/damiano1996/phosphene/security/advisories/new) (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability") and say it is a conduct report. Only the maintainers can read it. Reports are handled promptly and in confidence.
